# Data Availability

The **AiCumene EEG-BCI Sleep–Wake Dataset** has two components: a **public synthetic sleep-EEG benchmark** of **45 synthetic recordings** produced by per-recording oscillator resynthesis of Sleep-EDF Expanded (Sleep Cassette; PhysioNet) — phase-randomised surrogates preserving each source recording's oscillatory structure — and **40 real AiCumene wearable-EEG recordings** held under controlled access.

All **45 synthetic recordings**, together with associated public metadata, are archived on Zenodo:

**AiCumene EEG-BCI Sleep–Wake Dataset**  
**DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22923070**

The companion dataset repository containing documentation, figures, machine-readable citation metadata, reproducibility instructions, and controlled-access documentation is:

https://github.com/aicumene/aicumene-eeg-bci-sleep-wake-dataset

The original synthetic outputs, metadata, and documentation are licensed under **CC BY-NC 4.0** to the extent the creators are entitled to license them. Rights in the underlying Sleep-EDF source database remain governed by **ODC-BY 1.0**.

The **40 real AiCumene wearable-EEG recordings** are available to qualified researchers for non-commercial scientific research under controlled access and are not stored in the public GitHub repository or the public Zenodo deposit.

Controlled access is used to limit unrestricted dissemination of individual-level neurophysiological recordings and, where applicable, to maintain compliance with participant-consent, ethical, privacy, and data-protection requirements.

Requests for controlled access should be sent to:

**bernadotte@aicumene.com**

Requests should include the researcher's name, institutional affiliation, institutional email address, a brief description of the proposed research, and confirmation that the intended use is non-commercial. Complete requests will normally receive a decision within **10 business days**.

Approved requests are subject to a data-use agreement provided to the researcher as part of the controlled-access process.

Controlled access does **not** require scientific collaboration with the dataset creators, agreement with their interpretation of the data, or co-authorship on resulting publications. Researchers receiving access may independently analyse the recordings and publish confirming, non-replicating, contradictory, or otherwise independent scientific findings, subject to the Data Use Agreement and applicable participant-protection requirements.

Researchers using the dataset should cite:

**Bernadotte, A. & Menshikov, I. (2026). AiCumene EEG-BCI Sleep–Wake Dataset (Version 1.0). AiCumene. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22923070.**
