# Representative figures

This directory contains representative **illustrative figures derived from the public synthetic recordings** of the **AiCumene EEG-BCI Sleep–Wake Dataset**.

These figures are provided as **supplementary visual documentation**. They are not a substitute for the canonical raw public data, which are the EDF / EDF+ files archived in `public_data/`.

Dataset DOI: **10.5281/zenodo.22923070**  
Companion dataset repository: https://github.com/aicumene/aicumene-eeg-bci-sleep-wake-dataset  
Canonical BOID code repository: https://github.com/aicumene/BOID  
Associated BOID preprint: https://research.aicumene.com/papers/boid-coma/  
**DOI:** 10.13140/RG.2.2.31610.25288

Unless otherwise noted, figure copyright belongs to **Alexandra Bernadotte**.

## BOID figures

### `S0003_boid_sleep_profile.png`
**Figure. S0003 — oscillator dimensionality (BOID) across sleep stages.** Each point is the Brain Oscillator Intrinsic Dimension (BOID) — the number of active oscillatory modes (1–40 Hz) recovered from the two EEG derivations within a 30-s epoch — plotted over the sleep period and coloured by the concurrent sleep stage: **Wake (red), N1 (orange), N2 (light blue), N3 (dark blue), REM (purple)**. The black line is the running median; the background shading marks the sleep stage. The right panel summarises the per-stage BOID distribution (box = interquartile range, central line = median; Kruskal–Wallis test *p*). Oscillator dimensionality decreases with sleep depth: it is highest in wakefulness, declines through N1 → N2, reaches its minimum in slow-wave sleep (N3), and is intermediate in REM.

## Auxiliary PSG figures

### `S0003_auxiliary_psg_channels.png`
**Figure. S0003 — auxiliary PSG channels across sleep stages.** Top panel: hypnogram (sleep stages W, N1, N2, N3, REM on a 30-s epoch grid). Lower panels: submental electromyogram (EMG, µV), electro-oculogram (EOG, µV), respiration (Resp, a.u.) and body temperature (Temp, °C), each sampled over the sleep period. Background shading in the signal panels marks the concurrent sleep stage — **Wake (red), N1 (orange), N2 (light blue), N3 (dark blue), REM (purple)** — matching the hypnogram above. Muscle tone is stage-dependent: tonic EMG amplitude is highest in wakefulness, declines across NREM sleep (N1 → N3), and reaches its minimum (**muscle atonia**) during REM; body temperature varies smoothly within the physiological range (≈ 36.5–37.2 °C).
